Six working browser generators: jobs, person names, emails, passwords, multilingual words with English meanings, random numbers. 1–100,000 rows CSV. No API. Country selection includes 195 countries but bundled multilingual dictionary is limited to 15 languages; countries without a matching dictionary use a random available language. No claims of universal translation. The keypad opens on clicking Number of Records. Customize card images in assets/collections/01.svg through 06.svg or change paths in index.html.
