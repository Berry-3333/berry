Other Planet Language — generator #15

Select Other Planet Language in the generator selector. Choose Random Mix, Single Word, Short Sentence, Long Sentence, or Paragraph. Click Generate or Preview, then download CSV.GZ.

The fictional words have stable English meanings within a small built-in lexicon. Generated sentences are synthetic and repetitive at scale; the English meaning is a compositional interpretation, not a translation of a real language.

No planet names or external APIs. This generator runs entirely in the browser on Cloudflare Pages.
