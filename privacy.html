Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy describes the Zharief.com static website and its browser-based generator. Review and update this policy whenever you add analytics, advertising, forms, or other third-party services.

Information processed locally

Generator selections, ID ranges, previews, and generated CSV exports are processed in your browser. The current static generator does not require a user account or submit generated records to a Zharief database.

Hosting and technical logs

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request information, device details, and security logs to deliver and protect the site under their own policies.

Cookies and advertising

This version does not intentionally load third-party advertising scripts. If advertising is enabled later, we will update this policy to identify the relevant providers and describe cookies, similar technologies, consent options, and any applicable opt-out mechanisms. Advertising will not be enabled solely by publishing this page.

Downloads and external links

Downloads are initiated by your browser. External websites, if linked, have their own privacy practices.

Retention and rights

We do not maintain a user profile database for the current generator. Hosting providers may retain technical logs according to their policies. Where applicable, you may request information about personal data processed through our services.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details on our Contact page. The site owner should add a verified contact address before applying to advertising programs.
