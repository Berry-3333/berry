HOW TO REPLACE THE SIX CATEGORY IMAGES

Open index.html in VS Code and find assets/collections/01.svg through 06.svg.
Replace each img src with your own file path, e.g. assets/collections/job-ideas.webp.
Place your JPG, PNG, SVG or WEBP images inside assets/collections/.
