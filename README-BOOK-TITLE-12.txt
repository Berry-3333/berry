Zharief.com — Book Title Generator 12
====================================
Static Cloudflare Pages website. Upload the contents of the single parent folder.

Generator 12 is accessible through OPEN GENERATOR and the workspace dropdown.
Offline: book-title.js and generator.js run in the browser without APIs.
100,000 taxonomy paths = 25 major topics x 40 subtopics x 100 themes. These are generated combinations, NOT 100,000 independently curated categories.
Settings are inspirations, not guarantees of country-specific cultural accuracy.
Book titles may repeat or resemble existing books. Check originality, availability and trademarks before publication.
Browser CSV.GZ export supports up to 100,000 rows at a time and needs CompressionStream support.
All other generators are preserved from the previous version.
